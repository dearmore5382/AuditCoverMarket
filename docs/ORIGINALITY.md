# Mechanism comparison

| Dimension | EndorsementSplit | AgentSpendFirewall | AuditCover Market |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposition | relative contribution bands | invoice purpose within mandate | report finding answers an immutable question |
| Evidence | wallet claims and attestations | invoice/mandate records | independently published auditor article + finding |
| Intake | scoped claims and endorsements | spending intents | sealed price/locator commitments and timed reveals |
| AI boundary | contribution band/overlap | purpose classification | complete bid-to-question coverage relation |
| Deterministic derivation | fixed-weight allocation shares | budget authorization and consumption | full bounded minimum-cost set-cover enumeration |
| Persistence | one allocation snapshot | authorization ledger | auction bids, coverage matrix and payment liabilities |
| Consequence | non-custodial allocation record | spending permission | native GEN winner credits and unused-budget refund |
| Recovery | unresolved judgment | revoke authorization | expiry refund before clearing; immutable winner reservation after clearing |

The new mechanism purchases sufficient coverage rather than ranking contributors or authorizing invoice use. Price discovery and sealed offers are protocol inputs, while intelligence supplies the semantic incidence matrix. Deterministic optimization, budget reservation and parent/child transfer confirmation provide the economic boundary. This is a design comparison with known local projects, not a claim of universal novelty or guaranteed acceptance.
